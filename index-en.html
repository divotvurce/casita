How little it takes to find oneself again.

LA-MA HOME GLAMPING BRDY was created with the desire to build a place where there is no need to rush. A hidden place at the edge of the Brdy forest, far from the hustle of everyday life, where the morning begins in silence and the day can flow at its own pace.

We wanted to create a cozy self-sufficient space, where simplicity meets comfort and nature is a natural part of every moment. A place for slow mornings, long evenings, relaxation in the wellness area and moments when there is no need to think about what comes next.

LA-MA HOME GLAMPING BRDY produces its electricity from the sun and wind and stores it for moments when it is needed. We collect, filter, and reuse rainwater. We carefully sort waste, and wastewater management is also arranged so that its operation does not unnecessarily burden the surrounding nature. We strive to use mainly what the place and nature themselves offer, while treating it with respect.

It is a place for slow mornings, long evenings, wellness rest, and moments when you do not need to think about what comes next. Comfort here does not have to mean being separated from nature – on the contrary. We want to show that even a comfortable stay can work simply, considerately, and with as little impact on its surroundings as possible.

LA-MA HOME GLAMPING BRDY is a place where you can allow yourself to stop. It reminds us that time flows the same everywhere, but we can perceive it differently each time. And sometimes it's enough to have just silence, nature, a few worn-out lamps and someone with whom you want to share these moments.
